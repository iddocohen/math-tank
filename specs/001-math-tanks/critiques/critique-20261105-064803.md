# Critique Report: Math Tanks

**Date**: 2026-11-05
**Feature**: [spec.md](../spec.md)
**Plan**: [plan.md](../plan.md)
**Verdict**: ⚠️ PROCEED WITH UPDATES

## Executive Summary

The spec covers every PRD acceptance criterion and the plan has a sound core: engine-free, seeded logic in `src/core`, one `numberToX` mapping for the number line and the battlefield, answer-first challenge generation, and a single controller. Six items need fixing before `/speckit.tasks`. Three concern the guided turn itself: the "?" cloud shows where the answer is, the enemy and player tanks move every turn (which conflicts with the PRD's "learn from misses" and creates a circular dependency in `generateChallenge`), and finger-sized selection on a 7 to 9 CSS px unit spacing will turn motor slips into "wrong answers". The other three are a mastery rule that cannot be reached with adaptive difficulty on, no minimum spacing between comparison tanks, and Safari's 7-day storage eviction on iPad, a primary target device. All six are resolvable by editing spec.md, plan.md and data-model.md; none requires a different architecture.

## Engineering Lens Findings 🔬

### Architecture Soundness
| ID | Severity | Finding | Suggestion |
|----|----------|---------|------------|
| E1 | 🎯 | Mastery is effectively unreachable with adaptive difficulty on (the default). `recent` holds at most 10 outcomes and is cleared on every step change (data-model.md, Adaptive rule). A child who succeeds steps up after 5 of 6, so `recent` never reaches the 10 non-guess outcomes that `status()` needs, except at the top of the ladder. Guesses also take slots in `recent`. | Keep two windows per skill and range: an adaptive window (cleared on step change) and a mastery window of the last 10 non-guess attempts that is never cleared by step changes. Update FR-039 and data-model.md SkillProgress. |
| E2 | 💡 | One challenge counts toward several skills (FR-036), and `generateChallenge` receives the steps of all skills. Which skill's step drives a "43 − 7" challenge (subtraction or crossTens) is undefined. Label level is also part of each skill's ladder, so the number line's labeling can change from turn to turn depending on the skill. | Give each challenge kind one primary skill whose step drives generation; secondary skills are only recorded. Drive the label level from the `positions` skill only, so the number line looks the same across turns. |
| E3 | 💡 | Tick rules are undefined for custom spans between 101 and 999 and below 10. FR-016 covers only ranges up to 100 and 0 to 1000. Under research.md R8, a span of 150 gives a minor step of 10, equal to the major step, so no minor ticks appear. | Add a table of major and minor steps per span band to FR-016 and R8, and test `ticksFor` over every span from 5 to 1000. |
| E4 | 💡 | Zoomed number-line views (US9 two-step selection and help step 2) no longer sit directly under the battlefield (FR-015), because the battlefield mapping does not change (R7). The spec does not say how the child sees the link between the zoomed number and the battlefield (PRD 3.1). | Specify a visual link for zoomed views, for example bracket lines from the zoomed segment's end points to the matching battlefield span, and keep the guide line drawn from the battlefield position. |

### Failure Mode Analysis
| ID | Severity | Finding | Suggestion |
|----|----------|---------|------------|
| E5 | 💡 | `vite-plugin-pwa` with `registerType: 'autoUpdate'` (R12) can reload the page while a round is running when a new version activates. No storage migration from `v1` to a later version is defined. | Use `registerType: 'prompt'` and apply updates only on the start screen. Add a `migrate(version)` step to `core/storage` before Zod validation. |
| E6 | 💡 | Changing the range breaks dependent settings. `addition.maxResult` defaults to `range.max` and `subtraction.maxOperand` must be at most the span, so switching from 0 to 100 to 0 to 20 makes stored values invalid and disables the save button with errors the parent did not cause. | Clamp dependent fields automatically when the range changes; show validation errors only for values the parent entered. |

### Security & Privacy
| ID | Severity | Finding | Suggestion |
|----|----------|---------|------------|
| E7 | 🎯 | Safari and iPadOS delete all script-writable storage (localStorage, service worker caches) after 7 days without user interaction for sites not added to the Home Screen. On iPad, a primary target, settings and progress will silently reset after a week's break, breaking AC-08, FR-048 and SC-007. research.md R11 and R12 do not mention this. | Add to R11/R12: call `navigator.storage.persist()`, show "add to Home Screen" guidance in the parent menu, add export and import of settings and progress as a JSON file on the progress page, and add a WebKit project to the Playwright persistence and offline tests. |

### Performance & Scalability
| ID | Severity | Finding | Suggestion |
|----|----------|---------|------------|
| E8 | 💡 | 5,000 attempts at about 250 to 300 bytes of JSON each is 1.2 to 1.5 MB, which contradicts "stored data under 1 MB" (plan.md Scale/Scope). The whole progress object is serialized and written synchronously after each attempt, which can drop frames on a tablet (SC-002). | Cap attempts at about 1,000 or store them as compact tuples, keep aggregates in `SkillProgress`, and save between turns, never during a shot animation. |
