## Overview

Children aged 6 to 9 play a turn-based tank artillery game in the browser against a computer opponent. The battlefield's horizontal axis is a number line. To find out where the enemy is, the child solves a number-line, addition, subtraction or comparison challenge. The answer is a number, and the child aims by choosing that number: the cannon has a fixed angle, and the shell lands at the battlefield position of the chosen number. Every chosen number is fired. When the shell lands at the enemy's exact number, under the dashed pointer line, the enemy is destroyed and a new enemy appears; a shell on the tank body but not at the number only makes it shake. The child's tank has 3 hearts. The enemy is always visible, and it fires back after every shot of the child that does not destroy it; its shell usually lands just beside the child's tank and sometimes hits, depending on the difficulty (amendment 07). The score is the number of destroyed enemies, shown with the time on the end screen and compared only with the child's own personal best (amendment 06). When the shell lands off the tank, the game shows where it landed, and help becomes more explicit after each miss. Every child action works with number keys and Enter alone, as well as with mouse and touch. The parent can set any number range with a width from 5 to 1000, such as 550 to 780. The ruler along the bottom of the battlefield shows more or fewer lines and labels depending on its detail level, and in "read the ruler" challenges the child reads the number under a visible enemy.

The game is built for children with low frustration tolerance. It never uses punishing language or sounds, never takes away progress for a mathematical mistake, and always lets the child continue. A parent configures the mathematics and views the child's progress behind a gate that a child does not open by accident.

This release is the MVP described in PRD section 14, extended to cover every PRD acceptance criterion (AC-01 to AC-10). Cosmetic unlocks, advanced AI, detailed learning analytics and additional battlefield themes are deferred, as PRD section 14 states.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Play an artillery battle against the computer (Priority: P1)

The child starts a battle from the start screen. A side-view battlefield with rolling hills shows the child's tank and the enemy tank. In free play the enemy is visible, and the child reads the ruler to find the number under it. On their turn, the child chooses a number on the number line under the battlefield, by tapping, dragging or typing it, and fires. The cannon keeps a fixed angle; the game computes the arc so that the shell lands at the battlefield position of the chosen number. The shell explodes there and leaves a crater in the hill. When the chosen number is the enemy's number, the enemy is destroyed and a new enemy appears at a new position. Tanks settle onto the changed ground. After every shot of the child that does not destroy the enemy, the enemy fires back, also with a visible arc; its shell usually lands just beside the child's tank and sometimes hits, depending on the difficulty, and each enemy hit removes one of the child's 3 hearts. The round ends when the child's hearts are gone or, if the parent set a number of enemies per round, when the child has destroyed that many. The end screen shows how many enemies the child destroyed and how long the round took. *(Changed by amendment 06: one exact hit destroys the enemy, the child has 3 hearts, the enemy fires after every 2 shots, and the round counts destroyed enemies. Changed by amendment 07: the enemy fires back after every shot that does not destroy it.)* The child can start a new round with a new random landscape without reloading the page.

**Why this priority**: Without an entertaining game, no child will engage with the mathematics. This story alone is a playable game (AC-01).

**Independent Test**: Start the game in free-play mode, play a full round by choosing numbers and firing, and confirm that every shell lands at the chosen number, that craters, the destroyed enemy on the exact number, the shake on a near miss on the tank body, the enemy shot after every shot that does not destroy it, the hearts, the round end with score and time, and replay all work, and that the round can be played with the keyboard alone.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the game is loaded, **When** the child taps "Spielen" (Play), **Then** a battlefield with random rolling hills, the child's tank and the enemy tank appears within 3 seconds.
2. **Given** it is the child's turn, **When** the child chooses a number and taps the fire button or presses Enter, **Then** a shell flies along a visible ballistic arc from the fixed cannon and explodes at the battlefield position of that number. The child never sets an angle or a power, and the cannon cannot be dragged.
3. **Given** a shell explodes on a hill, **When** the explosion finishes, **Then** the hill has a crater at that spot, and any tank standing over removed ground moves down to rest on the new surface.
4. **Given** the child fires at the enemy's exact number, **When** the explosion finishes, **Then** the enemy is destroyed with a large explosion, the message "Getroffen!" appears, the tank counter at the top goes up by one, and a new enemy appears at a new position. **Given** the child fires at a number on the enemy tank's body but not at its number, **Then** the enemy tank shakes, is not destroyed, and the game shows "Ganz knapp! Triff die Linie." with both numbers and the step count. **Given** an enemy shell hits the child's tank, **Then** one of the 3 hearts above the child's tank goes out; an enemy shell next to the tank changes nothing. *(Changed by amendment 06: replaces health damage, the Volltreffer and the body hit of amendment 04.)*
5. **Given** the child's shot did not destroy the enemy, **When** the shell has landed, **Then** the enemy fires at once and its shell also follows a visible arc from the enemy tank. Its shell lands beside the child's tank, outside the tank's hit area, and comes closer with each further miss in a row (FR-098, FR-099); it hits when the child's misses in a row reach the difficulty's limit, and its first shot of a round always misses. **Given** the child destroys an enemy, **Then** the destroyed enemy does not fire, the hills change to a new landscape, a new enemy appears, and the child shoots next. *(Changed by amendment 06: the enemy no longer fires after every shot. Changed by amendment 07: the enemy fires after every shot that does not destroy it, and usually misses just beside the child's tank. Changed by amendment 13: the enemy zeroes in on misses in a row instead of hitting by chance, and a new landscape follows every destroyed enemy.)*
6. **Given** a shot has missed, **When** the child chooses the next number, **Then** the landing number is shown large, the path of the previous shot is still shown as a faint trail, and the distance from the landing number to the enemy is shown in steps on the number line, in every mode. *(Changed by amendment 07: before, only free play showed the distance.)*
7. **Given** a round has ended, **When** the child taps "Nochmal" (Again) or presses Enter, **Then** a new round starts with a different landscape, without a page reload.
8. **Given** the child's 3 hearts are gone, **When** the round ends, **Then** the game shows an encouraging message (for example "Gut gekämpft! Noch eine Runde?"), the number of destroyed enemies and the time played (for example "9 Panzer getroffen in 6:12"), never "verloren" (lost), and no learning progress is lost. *(Changed by amendment 06.)*
9. **Given** a hill stands between the cannon and the chosen number, **When** the child fires, **Then** the game raises the arc so that the shell still lands at the chosen number.

### User Story 2 - Find the enemy on the number line (Priority: P1)

In guided mode (the default), each search starts with a challenge, such as "Finde 37" (Find 37) or "Der Gegner ist bei 43 − 7", whose answer is the enemy's number. The enemy tank and its dashed pointer line to the ruler are visible from the start, so the child can solve the challenge or read the ruler; both practise the number line. *(Changed by amendment 07: before, the enemy was hidden until the challenge was solved.)* A number line spans the bottom of the screen and covers the same range as the battlefield, so position 37 on the number line is directly below position 37 on the battlefield. The child taps or drags on the number line, or types the number, to choose it and fires with the large fire button or the Enter key. The game draws a vertical guide line from the chosen number up to the battlefield and places a target marker there. Each fire is an answer. When the shell lands at the enemy's exact number, the enemy is destroyed. A shell on the enemy tank's body but not at its number makes the tank shake and counts as a miss. When it is wrong, the shell lands at the chosen number, the landing number is shown large, the ruler shows the step count from the landing number to the enemy, and help becomes more explicit (User Story 5). The enemy fires back after every shot of the child that does not destroy it (amendment 07).

After the hit, or after the child fires at the shown solution, a new enemy appears at a new position and a new search starts. A player turn is one search. The round ends when the child's 3 hearts are gone or, if the parent chose a number of enemies per round, when the child has destroyed that many. *(Changed by amendment 06: one hit destroys the enemy; the enemy fires after every 2 shots, so a miss can be followed by an enemy shot; the round counts destroyed enemies, not searches. Changed by amendment 07: the new enemy is visible, and the enemy fires after every shot that does not destroy it.)*

The number line shows major tick marks (for example 0, 10, 20 and so on) and minor tick marks for every unit in between, all equally spaced. Which lines and labels are visible depends on the ruler detail level (Stufe 1 to 5).

**Why this priority**: This is the primary learning goal of the PRD: understanding that positions match numbers, including what major and minor ticks mean (AC-02, AC-03).

**Independent Test**: With range 0 to 100 and only number-line challenges on, play searches and confirm that each chosen number places a marker at the matching battlefield position, that the shell lands there, that only the exact number destroys the enemy, that a shell on the tank body but not at the number is a miss with a shake, and that ticks between 30 and 40 are drawn and selectable.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a range of 0 to 100, **When** the child selects 37 on the number line, **Then** a guide line and a target marker appear at the battlefield position that matches 37, and the selected number is shown in large digits.
2. **Given** the number line between 30 and 40, **When** it is displayed, **Then** 30 and 40 have major ticks, 31 to 39 have minor ticks, and every tick is the same distance from its neighbors.
3. **Given** the challenge "Finde 37", **When** the child chooses 37 and fires, **Then** the shell lands at 37, the challenge counts as solved, and the enemy at 37 is destroyed. *(Changed by amendment 06. Changed by amendment 07: the enemy was visible all along.)*
4. **Given** a challenge of the type "Welche Zahl kommt direkt nach 49?" (which number comes right after 49), **When** the child fires at 50, **Then** the challenge counts as solved.
5. **Given** ruler level 1, **Then** major and minor lines are drawn and every line is labeled where space allows (otherwise only the typed number is labeled, under the marker). **Given** level 2, **Then** major and minor lines are drawn and only major lines are labeled. **Given** level 3, **Then** major and minor lines are drawn and only some major lines are labeled, always including the two end points. **Given** level 4, **Then** only major lines are drawn and labeled. **Given** level 5, **Then** only the two end points are drawn and labeled.
6. **Given** the child has selected a number, **When** the child moves their finger or pointer along the line, **Then** the guide line and the battlefield marker follow it, and no number is shown until the shot has been fired. Releasing only previews the number; the number is fired only when the child taps the fire button (which replaces the check button) or presses Enter. *(Changed by amendment 05: no number while pointing, and the +1 and −1 buttons are removed.)*
7. **Given** the challenge "Finde 37", **When** the child fires at 41, **Then** the shell lands at 41, the number 41 is shown large, the ruler shows the bracket from 41 to 37 with "4 Schritte", the enemy is not destroyed, the first help step appears, and the enemy fires back once. *(Changed by amendment 06: a miss no longer prevents the enemy's shot. Changed by amendment 07: the enemy is visible, the step count is shown, and the enemy fires after every shot.)*
8. **Given** a guided search, **When** the challenge is shown, **Then** the enemy tank and its dashed pointer line are visible, with no number drawn at the line, in every challenge kind. *(Changed by amendment 07: before, nothing showed the enemy until it was hit or the solution was shown.)*
9. **Given** the enemy has been hit at its exact number, **When** the explosion finishes, **Then** the enemy is destroyed, the hills change to a new landscape, a new enemy appears at a new position, and a new search starts; the destroyed enemy does not fire, and the child shoots next. *(Changed by amendment 06. Changed by amendment 07: the new enemy is visible. Changed by amendment 13: the new landscape, FR-101.)*
10. **Given** a "read the ruler" challenge, **When** it is shown, **Then** the enemy is visible and no number is given; **When** the child fires at the number under the enemy, **Then** the enemy is destroyed. **When** the child fires at a number on the enemy tank's body but not at its number, **Then** the enemy shakes, is not destroyed, and the ruler shows "Ganz knapp! Triff die Linie." with both numbers and the distance, for example "Du: 630 · Gegner: 637 · 7 Schritte". *(Changed by amendment 06.)*
11. **Given** any ruler level and a "read the ruler" challenge, **When** the child fires at a number off the enemy tank's body, **Then** it is a miss, whatever the visible lines. *(Changed by amendment 04: the read-the-ruler tolerance of amendment 02 is replaced by the tank-body rule. Changed by amendment 06: only the exact number destroys the enemy, on every level.)*
12. **Given** the enemy is visible, **Then** a thin dashed vertical line runs from the center of the enemy tank down to the ruler and ends in a small triangle on the ruler line, with no number drawn near it and no ruler label highlighted, and a small target ring marks the spot where the line meets the tank (amendment 06). This holds in every mode and every challenge kind. *(Changed by amendment 07: before, a guided position search showed no line until the enemy was revealed.)*

### User Story 3 - Addition and subtraction move along the number line (Priority: P2)

Some challenges are additions or subtractions whose result is the enemy's position, such as "Der Gegner ist bei 43 − 7" (The enemy is at 43 − 7). The number line shows the starting number. The child selects the result. When the child asks for help, or at the second help step after misses, a marker steps from the starting number to the right (addition) or to the left (subtraction), one unit at a time, and splits the movement at a ten boundary when one is crossed (for example 43 → 40 → 36).

**Why this priority**: Addition and subtraction are secondary objectives and part of the MVP (AC-05).

**Independent Test**: Turn on only subtraction, generate the exercise 43 − 7, use the step animation, and fire at 36.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the exercise 43 − 7, **When** the child taps the help button, **Then** a marker moves left from 43 to 40 and then from 40 to 36, counting each step aloud or on screen, and the child can then fire at 36.
2. **Given** the exercise 24 + 8, **When** the step animation runs, **Then** the marker moves right from 24 to 30 and then to 32.
3. **Given** crossing tens is turned off, **When** exercises are generated, **Then** no exercise crosses a ten boundary (for example 24 + 8 is never generated, but 24 + 5 can be).
4. **Given** negative results are turned off (the default), **When** subtraction exercises are generated, **Then** no result is below 0 or below the range minimum.
5. **Given** the parent sets maximum operand 10 and maximum result 20 for addition, **When** addition exercises are generated, **Then** no added number is above 10 and no result is above 20.

### User Story 4 - Compare numbers to find the enemy (Priority: P2)

Some turns ask a comparison question in the banner, such as "Der Gegner steht bei der größeren Zahl: 47 oder 74" (the enemy stands at the greater number: 47 or 74). The banner names two or three numbers, and one tank stands at each of them. The real enemy stands at the number that answers the question; the other tanks are decoys. All of them look like the enemy, each with its dashed pointer line and target ring, and none carries a number plate. The child works out the answer from the question, finds the tank at that number by reading the ruler, and then types the number and presses Enter, picks it on the ruler and fires, or taps the tank. A shot at a decoy bursts it like a cardboard dummy and counts as a miss. *(Changed by [amendment 09](./amendment-09-comparison-one-enemy.md): the decoys, their number plates, tank tapping and the order badges were removed. Changed by [amendment 10](./amendment-10-decoys-without-plates.md): the decoys and tank tapping are back, without number plates.)*

**Why this priority**: Comparison is a secondary objective and part of the MVP (AC-06).

**Independent Test**: Turn on only comparison and get the question "Der Gegner steht bei der größeren Zahl: 47 oder 74". Two identical tanks stand at 47 and 74. Firing at 47 bursts the decoy and counts as a miss; typing 74 and pressing Enter destroys the enemy.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the question "Der Gegner steht bei der größeren Zahl: 47 oder 74" and two identical tanks at 47 and 74, **When** the child types 74 and presses Enter, picks 74 on the ruler and fires, or taps the tank at 74, **Then** the cannon fires at 74, the enemy is destroyed, and the decoy at 47 folds away. *(Changed by amendments 09 and 10.)*
2. **Given** a "smaller" question such as "Der Gegner steht bei der kleineren Zahl: 18 oder 21", **When** the child fires at the smaller number, **Then** the challenge counts as solved. **When** the child fires at the other number, **Then** the decoy standing there bursts like a cardboard dummy with a light "plopp" sound, the shot is a miss, the first help step appears, and the enemy fires back, as after any miss. *(Changed by amendments 07, 09 and 10.)*
3. **Given** a "middle of three" question such as "Der Gegner steht bei der mittleren Zahl: 72, 27, 45" and one tank at each of the three numbers, **When** the child fires at 45, **Then** the challenge counts as solved. *(Added by amendment 09; replaces ordering three numbers by tapping. Changed by amendment 10.)*
4. *Removed by amendment 09.* The "equal" question gave its answer away, so the game no longer asks it.
5. **Given** a question such as "Der Gegner steht bei der Zahl näher an 50: 46 oder 57" or "Der Gegner steht bei der Zahl zwischen 30 und 40: 27, 35, 48", **When** the child fires at the matching number, **Then** the challenge counts as solved. *(Changed by amendment 09.)*
6. **Given** any comparison challenge, **Then** every number in the question is inside the configured range, the options are distinct, exactly one option is correct, the options are at least the minimum option gap apart and at least the minimum tank gap from the child's tank (FR-027), the enemy stands at the correct option and a decoy at each other option. *(Changed by amendments 09 and 10.)*
7. **Given** a decoy was destroyed in a search, **Then** it stays gone for the rest of that search. **Given** a shell lands on the body of any tank but not at its number, **Then** that tank shakes and the step count refers to the tank nearest the landing number, so it does not show which tank is the enemy. *(Added by amendment 10.)*

### User Story 5 - Gentle help after mistakes (Priority: P2)

Every fired number is an answer. When the shell lands at a wrong number, the game shows the landing number, responds with a friendly message, and never says "falsch" (wrong) or plays a negative sound. Help appears by itself after each miss and becomes more explicit: first the direction, then a count of the steps from the landing number to the answer, then the solution, which the child fires at with one tap or Enter. A miss never costs a heart by itself; the enemy fires back after every shot of the child that does not destroy it, and usually misses just beside the child's tank (amendment 07). A help button and the H key are always available and using them has no penalty. Answers that needed help are recorded as "with help", not as independent answers.

**Why this priority**: Low frustration is the critical design principle of the PRD (AC-07).

**Independent Test**: Fire at three wrong numbers in one search and confirm the help steps, that the enemy fires back after each of them, that the solution is shown and fired at with one tap or Enter, and that the answer is recorded as "with help".

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the challenge "Finde 37", **When** the child's first shot lands at 31, **Then** the game shows 31 large, an encouraging message such as "Versuch eine andere Stelle." (Try another spot), the direction "Weiter nach rechts" (further to the right), and highlights the nearest major tick to the answer.
2. **Given** a second miss, **Then** the game zooms into the ten-number interval that contains the answer and counts the steps from the landing number to the answer (for addition and subtraction, it shows the step animation instead).
3. **Given** a third miss, **Then** the game animates a marker to the correct number and shows it, and the child fires at it with one tap or Enter.
4. **Given** any challenge, **When** the child taps the help button or presses H before firing, **Then** the next help step is shown immediately, with no loss of hearts, shots or progress; a help request is not a shot and does not count toward the enemy's fire. *(Changed by amendment 06.)*
5. **Given** any challenge, **Then** no time limit is shown or enforced, unless the parent has turned one on. When a parent-set time limit runs out, the game shows the next help step, with no penalty.
6. **Given** the full set of on-screen and spoken texts, **Then** none contains discouraging words such as "falsch", "verloren" (lost) or "Fehler" (mistake) in feedback to the child.
7. **Given** a search with misses, **Then** the enemy fires back after each miss, as after any shot that does not destroy it, and the help steps continue; a miss itself never removes a heart. The enemy's shell comes closer with each miss in a row and hits when the misses in a row reach the difficulty's limit (FR-099); firing at the shown solution at help step 3 destroys the enemy and starts the count again, so a child who uses help can always avoid losing a heart. Asking for help never changes the enemy's shot. *(Changed by amendment 06: before, a miss never gave the enemy a turn. Changed by amendment 07: the enemy fires after every such shot. Changed by amendment 13: the enemy's hits follow the misses in a row.)*
8. **Given** the parent has turned "Punkt nach voller Hilfe" (point after the full help) off and a search shows the solution (help step 3), **When** the child destroys the enemy, **Then** a new enemy appears on new hills and the feedback line shows "Getroffen, mit Hilfe." (hit, with help), but the tank counter, the stars, the count toward "Gegner pro Runde" and the personal best do not change. A hit after help step 1 or 2 counts as usual. With the setting on (the default), a hit after help step 3 counts like any other hit. *(Added by amendment 18.)*

### User Story 6 - Parent settings that persist (Priority: P2)

A parent opens the settings by pressing and holding the gear icon for 2 seconds. The parent chooses which challenge types are on, the number range, operand limits, tens and hundreds crossing, negative results, the ruler's starting level and hardest allowed level (with a preview of the ruler), enemy difficulty, assistance options, sound and music, how a round ends (when the child's hearts are gone, or after a number of destroyed enemies), adaptive difficulty, free-play mode, the no-damage mode, whether a clock is shown during play, and whether the child's record is shown on the end screen. *(Changed by amendment 06.)* Settings take effect at the start of the next round and are kept after the browser is closed and reopened.

**Why this priority**: Parents need to match the game to the child's level, and the MVP requires parent configuration and local persistence (AC-04, AC-08).

**Independent Test**: Change several settings, close and reopen the browser, and confirm they are kept. Set range 0 to 20 and confirm that no generated exercise needs an answer outside 0 to 20.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the child taps the gear icon briefly, **Then** settings do not open and the game continues. **Given** a parent presses and holds it for 2 seconds, **Then** the game pauses and settings open.
2. **Given** the parent changes the range to 0 to 20 and enemy difficulty to "Leicht" (Easy), **When** the browser is closed and reopened, **Then** both settings are kept.
3. **Given** a range of 0 to 20, **When** any number of exercises are generated with any combination of enabled challenge types, **Then** every answer, every operand, every shown number and every intermediate step is between 0 and 20.
4. **Given** the parent turns off every challenge type, **Then** the settings screen shows that guided mode needs at least one challenge type and offers free-play mode instead; it never saves a configuration that cannot generate a challenge.
5. **Given** the parent enters a custom range where the minimum is not smaller than the maximum, **Then** the settings screen does not accept it and explains why.
6. **Given** settings changed during a round, **Then** the current round continues with the old settings and the new ones apply from the next round.
7. *Removed by amendment 06.* The "Genau treffen" (aim exactly) setting no longer exists, because only the exact number destroys the enemy in every round.
8. **Given** the parent chooses "Gegner pro Runde" with 3, **When** the child destroys the third enemy, **Then** the round ends with the end screen. **Given** the parent keeps the default "Bis alle Herzen weg" (until all hearts are gone), **Then** new enemies keep coming until the child's 3 hearts are gone. *(Added by amendment 06.)*
9. **Given** the parent turns on the no-damage mode, **Then** "Bis alle Herzen weg" is not offered and the round ends after the chosen number of enemies. *(Added by amendment 06.)*
10. **Given** the parent turns on "Uhr beim Spielen zeigen" (show the clock during play), **Then** an up-counting clock is shown at the top during play; with the default (off), the time is shown only on the end screen. **Given** the parent turns off "Rekord zeigen" (show the record), **Then** the end screen shows neither "Neuer Rekord!" nor "Dein Rekord". *(Added by amendment 06.)*
11. **Given** the parent opens "Spielstand übertragen" (transfer save) on the settings page, **Then** a QR code and a "Link kopieren" (copy link) button for the current save are shown. **When** the link is opened in another browser, **Then** a dialog asks "Spielstand übernehmen? Sterne: N, Rekord: M, Zahlenraum: A bis B", and only pressing and holding "Übernehmen" (take over) for 2 seconds replaces that browser's settings, stars, look, personal bests and progress summary. *(Added by amendment 15.)*
12. **Given** the dialog of scenario 11, **When** the parent chooses "Abbrechen" (cancel), **Then** nothing changes; in both cases the address no longer contains `#save=`. **Given** a damaged or foreign code, **Then** the game shows "Dieser Code passt nicht" and changes nothing. *(Added by amendment 15.)*

### User Story 7 - Difficulty adapts and parents view progress (Priority: P3)

The game tracks results separately for each skill: number positions, counting forward, counting backward, understanding tens, crossing tens, addition, subtraction and comparison. After repeated independent successes in a skill, it gradually raises difficulty within the parent's limits. When the child struggles, it lowers difficulty and offers help earlier. Answers that are too fast to be real thinking, or bursts of rapid random taps, do not count as success. A progress page behind the parent gate shows attempts, independent correct answers and answers with help per skill and over sessions.

**Why this priority**: Adaptive difficulty and the progress view are PRD requirements (AC-09), but the game is useful without them.

**Independent Test**: Simulate a sequence of independent correct answers and a sequence of wrong answers for one skill, and confirm difficulty goes up and down within the parent's limits. Open the progress page and confirm the counts.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** adaptive difficulty is on, **When** the child answers 3 challenges in a row in a skill correctly without help (guesses do not count and do not break the row), **Then** the next challenges in that skill are one difficulty step harder, but never beyond the parent's limits. *(Changed by amendment 17: before, 5 of the last 6.)*
2. **Given** adaptive difficulty is on, **When** the child needs help or does not solve 2 of the last 3 challenges in a skill, **Then** the next challenges are one difficulty step easier, but never below the easiest step. *(Changed by amendment 17: before, 3 of the last 5.)*
3. **Given** a correct answer confirmed less than 1.5 seconds after answers became possible, or a correct answer that follows 3 or more wrong answers within 5 seconds, **Then** it is recorded as a guess and does not count toward raising difficulty or mastery.
4. **Given** a skill where all correct answers were given with help, **Then** the progress page never shows that skill as mastered.
5. **Given** the parent opens the progress page, **Then** it shows, per skill, the number of attempts, independent correct answers and answers with help, the current difficulty step and how many more correct answers in a row raise it (amendment 17), a mastered or developing status per number range, the skills with the most help needed, and a per-session history.
6. **Given** adaptive difficulty is off, **Then** difficulty stays at the levels the parent configured.
7. *Removed by amendment 06.* There is no outcome close: a shell on the enemy tank's body that is not at the exact number is a miss.
8. **Given** the parent opens the progress page, **Then** it lists the child's personal best per number range, ruler starting level and round type, with the time of that round. *(Added by amendment 06.)*

### User Story 8 - Play anywhere, offline, on a tablet or a phone (Priority: P3)

The child plays with a mouse, a touch screen or the keyboard alone, on a desktop, a tablet or a phone held sideways, in fullscreen if desired. On touch devices the child types numbers on an on-screen number pad. *(Changed by amendment 12: phones and the number pad are added.)* With the keyboard, the child needs only number keys and Enter. ### Edge Cases

- **Range too small for a challenge type**: in range 0 to 10 with crossing tens off, some exercise types (such as "midpoint" or addition crossing ten) cannot be generated. The generator must skip those types for that configuration and never produce an impossible or out-of-range exercise. If no type is possible, the game falls back to "Finde N" challenges.
- **Enemy position overlaps the child's tank**: the child's tank stands at one end of the battlefield for the whole round (the end alternates between rounds), and answers that would place any tank closer than the minimum gap to the child's tank are not generated.
- **Comparison options too close together**: options must be at least the minimum option gap apart, which is at least the minimum tank gap, and at least the minimum tank gap from the child's tank; when the range cannot fit the options of a kind, that kind is skipped for that configuration (amendment 10).
- **Finger slip on a dense number line**: releasing never fires, so a slip only changes the preview.
- **Browser deletes stored data after a long break** (Safari on iPad deletes site data after 7 days without a visit unless the game is on the Home Screen): the game asks the browser for persistent storage, the settings page explains how to add the game to the Home Screen, and parents can export and import settings and progress as a file.
- **Game update available mid-round**: updates are applied only on the start screen, never during a round.
- **Shell leaves the screen**: the player's shell always lands at the chosen number (FR-065), so only an enemy shell can leave the left or right edge; it counts as a miss and its landing point is shown at the edge.
- **Hill blocks the fixed arc**: the game raises the cannon angle in 5-degree steps up to 85 degrees until the arc clears the hill; the shell always lands at the chosen number.
- **Typed number outside the range**: it is not fired; the preview stays at the nearest end of the range, and a friendly hint shows the range. Digits beyond the length of the longest number in the range are ignored.
- **Minus key in a range without negative numbers**: the key is ignored.
- **Keys pressed while a shell is in flight**: digits and Enter are ignored until the child can choose the next number, so a held Enter key never fires twice.
- **Comparison: a number that is not the answer**: *Changed by amendments 09 and 10.* A shot at a decoy's number bursts the decoy and is a miss; a shot at any other number is a miss like in every other challenge. There are no number plates and no order badges.
- **Near miss on the enemy**: a shell on the enemy tank's body but not at its number makes the tank shake and shows "Ganz knapp! Triff die Linie." with the step count; it does not destroy the enemy and counts as a miss. A shell off the tank body is a plain miss. *(Changed by amendment 06.)*
- **Narrow ranges**: in 0 to 10 the tank body is narrower than one step, so only the exact number lands on it; in 0 to 1000 the body covers about 40 numbers.
- **Tank on a destroyed slope**: when the ground under a tank is removed, the tank drops to the new surface and is never left floating or buried.
- **Crater digs through the bottom**: terrain never becomes lower than a fixed floor, so tanks always have ground.
- **Rapid tapping or typing**: fires before the challenge has been visible for 1 second are ignored and do not count as answers.
- **Settings opened mid-round**: the round pauses; on closing, it resumes with the old settings (see User Story 6, scenario 6).
- **Stored data is missing or damaged**: the game starts with default settings, keeps playing, and tells the parent on the settings page that settings were reset.
- **Speech not available in the browser**: the speaker icon is hidden and the game works without speech.
- **Browser storage full or blocked**: the game keeps working for the session and tells the parent on the settings page that settings cannot be saved.
- **Free-play with no challenge types enabled**: free play works without challenges (it never shows one).
- **The enemy's first shot of a round**: it always lands beside the child's tank and never takes a heart, on every difficulty, even when the misses in a row already reached the limit (two fast guesses on "Herausfordernd"). The count is kept, so the next miss brings the hit. *(Added by amendment 07. Changed by amendment 13.)*
- **The child's tank stands at the edge of the battlefield**: the enemy's deliberate near miss lands on the side of the tank that is inside the battlefield. *(A decision of this update; amendments 07 and 13 say "on either side" and "on a random side".)*
- **A fast guess jumps over the warning**: when a fast guess adds 2 to the misses in a row (FR-098), the count can skip the number that shows the warning (FR-100); the enemy then hits without a warning before it. *(A decision of this update for amendment 13.)*
- **Free play and the fast-guess rule**: free play has no attempt records and no help steps, so every miss there adds 1 to the misses in a row. *(A decision of this update for amendment 13, which names the FR-038 rule only for searches.)*
- **Number of enemies reached**: with "Gegner pro Runde", the round ends right after the last enemy is destroyed, before the enemy could fire again; the end screen names no winner or loser. *(Changed by amendment 06.)*
- **No-damage mode and "Bis alle Herzen weg"**: the combination would never end, so the settings page does not offer it, and stored settings that combine them are changed to "Gegner pro Runde" when loaded. *(Added by amendment 06.)*
- **Game paused, settings open or tab hidden**: the time of the round stops and continues when play continues. *(Added by amendment 06.)*
- **"Neue Landschaft" or "Zum Start" during a round**: the unfinished round ends without a result, so it is not compared with the personal best. *(A decision of this update; amendment 06 does not say.)*
- **Child destroys no enemy**: the end screen shows "0 Panzer getroffen" with the time and an encouraging message; a round with 0 destroyed enemies is never a new record. *(A decision of this update.)*

- **Stars after leaving a round early**: each star is stored when its enemy is destroyed, so "Zum Start", a closed tab or a reload never takes stars away; the end screen of the next finished round names only the items that round unlocked. *(A decision of this update; amendment 11 does not say.)*
- **All items unlocked**: after the last item (130 stars), stars keep counting and the end screen shows only the star total. *(A decision of this update.)*
- **Imported file without garage data** (exported before amendment 11): the stars and the chosen look of this browser are kept, because stars are never lost. *(A decision of this update.)*
- **Phone turned upright during a round** (amendment 12): the round pauses and the picture "Bitte dreh das Handy" shows until the phone is held sideways again; the parent can still open the settings and progress pages through the gear.
- **Number pad and keyboard together** (amendment 12): a tablet with a keyboard case shows the number pad on a narrow or touch screen; the pad and the keys both type into the same number.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

**Battlefield and shooting**

- **FR-001**: The game MUST show a side-view battlefield with randomly generated rolling hills, one player tank and one computer tank, with a new landscape for each round.
- **FR-002**: The computer MUST fire once after every shot of the player that does not destroy the enemy (FR-081). In guided mode a player turn is one search (FR-010a); a miss during a search does not end the player's turn (FR-013). *(Changed by amendment 06: turns no longer alternate shot by shot. Changed by amendment 07: the enemy fires after every such shot, not after every 2.)*
- **FR-003**: The player MUST aim by choosing a number, never by setting an angle or a power. The player chooses the number on the number line (FR-019) or by typing it (FR-060), and fires with a single large fire button or the Enter key. The cannon MUST NOT be draggable. *(Changed by amendment 01: angle and power controls removed.)*
- **FR-004**: Shells MUST follow a visible ballistic arc under constant gravity. The player's shell MUST land at the battlefield position of the chosen number (FR-065). The arc of the player's previous shot MUST stay visible as a faint trail until the next shot or until the ground changes (new hills after a destroyed enemy, "Neue Landschaft", a new round; product-owner bug report B1), and the landing number MUST be shown large after each player shot. After a miss the landing number stays until the next shot; after a hit it fades out after about 2 seconds, and it is removed at once when a later challenge starts. *(Timing added in Phase 22b, product owner report P7.)*
- **FR-005**: An explosion MUST remove terrain in a round area, leaving a crater; terrain MUST never drop below a fixed floor.
- **FR-006**: An enemy shell that lands on the player's tank body (within half the tank's drawn width of its center, measured along the battlefield) or hits the tank directly MUST remove one heart (FR-080); an enemy shell next to the tank MUST change nothing. The player's shell destroys the enemy only at its exact number (FR-066). *(Changed by amendment 04. Changed by amendment 06: no health or distance-based damage.)*
- **FR-007**: After terrain changes, tanks MUST settle onto the new ground surface.
- **FR-008**: A round MUST end when the player's 3 hearts are gone, or, with the round type "Gegner pro Runde", when the player has destroyed the configured number of enemies (FR-082). The end screen MUST show only encouraging text, the score and the time (FR-085), and MUST offer a new round without a page reload. *(Changed by amendment 06: no health, no turn count.)*
- **FR-009**: Explosions and hits MUST have clear visual effects; sound effects and music MUST be optional and controlled by settings. The game MUST NOT play loud or negative sounds in response to a wrong answer.

**Guided and free-play modes**

- **FR-010**: The game MUST offer guided mode, the default, in which each search for the enemy begins with a challenge whose solution is the number to fire at: either a battlefield position (number-line, addition and subtraction challenges) or the correct option of the question, where the real enemy stands among identical decoys (comparison challenges, amendment 10). Each fired number MUST count as an answer. The enemy tank and its pointer line (FR-073) MUST be visible in every challenge kind from the moment the enemy appears; the challenge stays the task, and the child may solve it or read the ruler. *(Changed by amendment 07: before, the enemy's position was hidden during a position search until the enemy was hit or the solution was shown.)*
- **FR-010a**: The player's tank MUST stand at one end of the battlefield for a whole round; the end MUST alternate between rounds. A player turn MUST be one search: it ends when the player's shell hits the enemy at its exact number, or when the player fires at the shown solution. Then the enemy is destroyed, a new enemy appears, visible, at a new position, and a new search starts. *(Changed by amendment 01: removed the rule that a found enemy stays for 3 shots, and the bonus challenge in follow-up turns. Changed by amendment 06: the hit destroys the enemy, and the enemy's fire follows FR-081 instead of following each search. Changed by amendment 07: the new enemy no longer hides.)*

- **FR-012**: *Removed by amendment 01.* Aim correction no longer exists, because the fixed cannon and the automatic arc (FR-065) always land the shell at the chosen number.
- **FR-013**: A miss MUST NOT end the search or remove a heart by itself; the enemy fires back after it (FR-081). *(Changed by amendment 06: before, a miss never gave the enemy a turn. Changed by amendment 07: the enemy fires after every shot that does not destroy it.)* After a miss, the shell lands at the chosen number, the landing number is shown large, the ruler shows the step count from the landing number to the enemy (FR-072), and the next help step appears (FR-031). After the help sequence shows the solution, the child MUST be able to fire at it with one tap or the Enter key.

### Key Entities

- **Settings**: the parent's configuration (see FR-046). One active set per browser.
- **Challenge**: one exercise for one player turn: its type, the numbers shown, the expected answer, the target it marks, the skills it counts toward, and the seed that produced it.
- **Attempt**: the record of one challenge as the child answered it (see FR-035).
- **Skill progress**: per skill and number range: current difficulty step, recent attempts used for adaptive rules, and mastered or developing status.
- **Session**: one period of play with its date and attempt totals. A new session starts when the game is opened, after 30 minutes without input, or on a new calendar day.
- **Round**: one battle: landscape, tanks, the child's hearts, the number of destroyed enemies (the score), the time played, the child's misses in a row, and outcome. *(Changed by amendment 06. Changed by amendment 07: the count of the child's shots since the enemy last fired is removed. Changed by amendment 13: the misses in a row, and a new landscape after every destroyed enemy.)*
- **Tank**: player, enemy or decoy (comparison turns only); position on the battlefield (as a number in the range) and whether it is destroyed. Tanks carry no number plate. Tanks have no health; the child's hearts belong to the round. *(Changed by amendments 06, 09 and 10.)*
- **Personal best**: per number range, ruler starting level and round type: the most enemies destroyed in one round and the time of that round (amendment 06).
- **Garage**: the child's star total and the chosen look (color, flag, trail, hat), kept apart from the learning progress. The unlocked items follow from the star total (amendment 11).
- **Garage item**: one tank color, flag, shell trail or hat, with its kind and the star total that unlocks it; the standard item of each kind needs no stars (amendment 11).
- **Transfer code**: a compact, versioned copy of the settings, the garage, the personal bests and the progress summary without the attempt history, carried in a link's fragment and shown as a QR code (amendment 15).

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

- **SC-001**: A child can start a battle from the start screen with at most 2 taps, and the battlefield appears within 3 seconds on a typical tablet.
- **SC-002**: During battle, animation runs at a steady rate with no visible stutter on a typical tablet (at least 30 frames per second on a 2020 or later mid-range tablet, 60 on desktop). *(Amendment 14: the textured ground is drawn once per landscape and per crater, never in every frame, FR-107. - **SC-003**: For each preset range and for custom ranges, 10,000 generated challenges per configuration produce no answer, operand, shown number or intermediate step outside the range, and no exercise that breaks a parent setting.
- **SC-004**: For every number in a range up to 0 to 100, selecting it on the number line places the battlefield marker within half a unit's width of the matching battlefield position.
- **SC-005**: In every search, the child can always fire at the enemy's number within at most 3 misses or 3 help requests, and a miss never removes a heart by itself. *(Changed by amendment 06: the enemy fires after every 2 shots, misses included. Changed by amendment 07: the enemy fires after every shot that does not destroy it.)*
- **SC-006**: No child-facing message, spoken text or sound in the game contains discouraging wording or a negative sound effect, as checked against the forbidden word list in FR-033.
- **SC-007**: Settings and progress are identical after closing and reopening the browser in 100% of test runs.
- **SC-008**: After one full load, a round can be started and finished with the network disconnected.
- **SC-009**: In guided mode, every player shot is fired at a number the child chose (on the number line, by typing, or by choosing a numbered tank) or at the shown solution, and the enemy is destroyed only when that number equals the enemy's number, so the number line is part of every search. *(Changed by amendment 06.)*
- **SC-010**: In observation sessions with target children, the child voluntarily starts another round in most sessions, and uses fewer help steps over successive sessions for the same skill and range (PRD section 15). These are evaluation goals for parents and testers, not automated tests.
- **SC-011**: A full guided round, from the start screen through searches, misses, help, the solution step, pause and resume, to the round end and a new round, can be played with only number keys, Enter, Escape, H and Backspace, with no mouse click or touch, in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox.
- **SC-012**: For every number in each preset range and on seeded terrains, the player's shell lands within half a unit's width of the chosen number's battlefield position.
- **SC-013**: For every range width from 5 to 1000 and starting points from -1000 to 9000, including 550 to 780, the ruler follows FR-016 and FR-017 at each of the 5 levels and shows at most 101 lines, and 10,000 generated challenges per tested range stay inside it.

## Assumptions

- **Scope**: the release covers the PRD's MVP list (section 14) and every acceptance criterion (AC-01 to AC-10). Ranges 0 to 50, 0 to 1000 and custom ranges are included as a P2 story (amendment 02).
- **Deferred, per PRD section 14**: *Changed by amendment 11:* new tank appearances, projectile effects and stars are built as the tank garage (US10). Still deferred: achievement badges, advanced AI, detailed learning analytics beyond FR-040, and additional battlefield themes. The PRD marks all rewards as optional.
- **Guided-mode design**: each challenge's answer is the enemy's position (in comparison, the correct option of the question), and aiming is choosing that number (amendment 01). The PRD offers "selecting a target coordinate" as one valid design.
- **Comparison tanks stand at their own numbers**, so a larger number is always further right. This keeps one consistent mapping between numbers and battlefield positions (PRD section 3.1) and reinforces the number line idea, even though a child can answer some comparisons by position.
- **Hosting**: the game is served as static files over HTTPS (for example GitHub Pages) or from localhost; offline mode does not work when the files are opened directly from disk.
- **Custom range limits**: a custom range has a width from 5 to 1000 and a minimum from -1000 to 9000, so the largest number has at most 4 digits plus a sign. The amendment gives no upper limit; this one keeps typed numbers short.
- **Read-the-ruler tolerance on level 5**: *Superseded by amendment 04.* Amendment 02's tolerance (half of the smallest visible step, the major step on level 5) no longer exists; the tank-body rule of FR-066 applies on every level.
- **Free play and progress**: free-play shots (always "read the ruler") are practice and are not recorded as attempts, so they do not change difficulty or mastery. The amendments do not say.
- **Parent gate**: press and hold for 2 seconds. This is a common pattern for apps for young children and needs no reading.
- **Help thresholds**: three escalation steps, with the solution shown on the third miss. The PRD asks for "progressively more explicit assistance" without numbers.
- **Adaptive thresholds** (5 of 6 to raise, 3 of 5 to lower, 1 second for guesses, 8 of 10 for mastery) are reasonable defaults; the PRD gives no numbers.
- **Round length**: *Changed by amendment 06.* The round length counts destroyed enemies ("Gegner pro Runde", 1 to 20, default 5) and applies only to that round type; the default round type is "Bis alle Herzen weg". When stored version 3 settings are loaded, the old turn count is kept as the number of enemies, limited to 1 to 20 (a decision of this update; the amendment does not say).
- **Hit damage**: *Superseded by amendment 06.* There is no damage: one exact hit destroys the enemy, and one enemy hit removes one of the child's 3 hearts.
- **Enemy hit on the child's tank**: an enemy shell hits when it enters the tank's hit circle or lands within half the tank's drawn width of the tank's center, the same body width as the child's near miss (a decision of this update; amendment 06 says only "if the enemy tank hits him").
- **New record**: a round is a new record only when it destroys more enemies than the stored best; the same number in a shorter time is not, and a round with 0 destroyed enemies never is (a decision of this update; amendment 06 says "the most enemies destroyed in one round, with its time"). The free-play and guided rounds share one best per range, starting level and round type, because the amendment names only those three.
- **Tank body width**: the hit area is the 60-unit tank width used for the tank gaps (FR-027), centered on the tank's number. In numbers it is 60 divided by the 1440-unit ruler length, times the range width: about 4 numbers in 0 to 100, about 9.6 in 550 to 780 and about 42 in 0 to 1000. The amendment says "about 4" and "about 40".
- **Comparison and the hit rule**: *Changed by amendment 10.* A comparison turn has the enemy at the correct option and a decoy at each other option. Only the enemy's exact number destroys the enemy; a decoy's exact number bursts that decoy as a miss; a shell on any tank's body but not at its number shakes that tank (FR-066).
- **Miss distance in guided mode**: *Superseded by amendment 07.* The enemy is always visible, so every miss shows the landing number and the step count to the enemy, in every mode; help step 1 adds the direction.
- **Enemy near-miss side**: the side of the deliberate near miss comes from the same seeded draw as the hit decision; when one side would leave the battlefield, the other side is used (a decision of this update).
- **Free-play enemy**: after each hit in free play, the destroyed enemy is replaced by a new enemy at a new visible position at least the minimum enemy gap from the player.
- **Battlefield mapping**: the battlefield's horizontal axis is the configured range, mapped linearly, and tanks stand at whole-number positions.
- **Devices**: *Changed by amendment 12.* "Typical tablet" means an iPad or Android tablet from 2020 or later; "phone" means an iPhone or Android phone from 2020 or later, played held sideways. The smallest tested screen is 844 by 390 CSS pixels (a phone held sideways); the tested sizes are 1024 by 768, 768 by 1024, 844 by 390 and, for the "Bitte dreh das Handy" picture, 390 by 844. The amendment names example sizes and no minimum; this is a decision of this update. *Changed by amendment 16:* Amazon Fire tablets in Silk are typical tablets too; their tested sizes are listed in FR-125, the smallest being 960 by 472 sideways and 600 by 832 upright.
- **Languages**: German only in this release; English is prepared by the translation table (FR-056) but not written.
- **Graphics and sound** are original, simple and drawn by the game; no assets from existing games are used. *(Changed by amendment 14:* one tileable dirt texture under CC0, "dirt" from "4 hand-painted ground textures" by Drummyfish (https://opengameart.org/node/84103), is bundled for the ground; everything else is still drawn by code. The texture was chosen because its OpenGameArt page states CC0 and a 512 by 512 tile; the amendment lists that page as one of four candidates. The CIE76 limit of 30 in FR-109 is a decision of this update: a difference of about 2.3 is just noticeable, and 30 keeps two colors clearly different at a glance, also for the darker shadow shades.)*
- **Transfer size and QR code**: *Added by amendment 15.* The limit of 2300 bytes for the whole link keeps it below the 2331 bytes that the largest QR code (version 40) holds with error correction M. Level M is used for every size (the amendment allows M or L). The QR code is drawn with the library uqr 0.1.3 (MIT license, https://github.com/unjs/uqr), bundled with the game. The transfer adds the guesses per skill, which the amendment does not list, because the progress page shows them (FR-040). The order of the session totals and the mastery windows that are shortened comes from the amendment; the rule for a save that is still too large after both steps is a decision of this update.
